# Workforce Planning & Succession — Workday County HR Reference

## Overview

Workforce planning answers three questions: what work the county will need done in the next
three to five years, who will be available to do it, and what to do about the gap. For many
counties the biggest single driver is retirement eligibility in long-tenure, hard-to-fill
jobs (public safety, skilled trades, engineering, finance, IT). Planning has to fit the
budget cycle, because every position change runs through position control.

---

## The Planning Cycle

| Phase | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Supply analysis | 6 months before budget | Headcount, retirement eligibility, turnover by job family |
| Demand analysis | With department heads | Service changes, new programs, grant-funded positions ending |
| Gap analysis | 5 months before budget | Positions at risk; skills gaps; hard-to-fill list |
| Strategy | 4 months before budget | Hiring plan, succession plans, training, reclassifications |
| Budget requests | Budget cycle | Position adds, reclasses, pay adjustments — see references/hr-budget-finance.md |
| Review | Quarterly | Progress against plan |

## Supply Analysis — Workday Data

| Measure | How |
|---|---|
| Headcount and FTE by job family and department | Headcount by Organization report |
| Age and service distribution | Worker report (aggregate only) |
| Retirement eligibility in 1, 3, 5 years | Requires plan eligibility rules — for **Missouri** LAGERS counties, use LAGERS eligibility criteria for your benefit program `[VERIFY: current LAGERS normal and early retirement eligibility rules]` |
| Turnover by job family | Terminations report ÷ average headcount |
| Time to fill by job family | Recruiting report |
| Internal bench | Employees in feeder jobs with required certifications |

**Privacy:** report age and retirement eligibility in aggregate. Never use age or retirement
eligibility to make an individual employment decision — that creates age discrimination risk
(ADEA applies to counties regardless of size).

## Critical Position Identification

Score each position 1–3 on each factor:

| Factor | 1 | 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Impact of vacancy on public service or safety | Low | High |
| Time to fill | Under 60 days | Over 6 months |
| Specialized license or knowledge | None | Rare / long training |
| Single incumbent | Several incumbents | Only one person can do it |
| Retirement or turnover risk | Low | High |

Positions scoring 12+ of 15 are critical. Every critical position needs a succession plan.

---

## Succession Planning

For each critical position:

1. Document key duties, decisions, systems, relationships and calendar (the "desk manual")
2. Identify ready-now, ready-in-1–2-years and longer-term internal candidates
3. Build development plans for candidates (training, stretch assignments, acting assignments)
4. Plan knowledge transfer: overlap period, shadowing, recorded walkthroughs
5. Review twice a year

**Government note:** civil service rules usually require competitive selection. Succession
planning develops candidates; it does not pre-select them. Acting or interim appointments
must follow the rules for temporary assignments and pay.

- Talent > Succession Plans: link candidates to positions, readiness and notes

## Strategies to Close Gaps

| Gap | Options |
|---|---|
| Retirement wave in one job family | Overlap hiring (budget approval), trainee classifications, knowledge transfer, phased retirement where the plan allows `[VERIFY: LAGERS return-to-work and phased retirement rules]` |
| Hard-to-fill technical jobs | Market pay review (references/compensation.md), apprenticeships, tuition assistance, partnerships with community colleges |
| High first-year turnover | Onboarding and supervisor fixes (references/onboarding.md), realistic job previews |
| Grant-funded positions ending | Plan transition or end date early; communicate with employees |
| Seasonal peaks | Seasonal and temporary workforce — references/seasonal-temp-workforce.md |

## Presenting to the Board

- One page: top 5 workforce risks, cost of inaction, recommended actions, budget impact
- Use aggregate data only
- Tie each request to a service outcome (response times, permit turnaround, road miles maintained)

See references/hr-director-playbook.md for the board report format.

## Workforce Planning KPIs

| KPI | Target | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Critical positions with a succession plan | 100% | Succession plan log |
| Critical positions with a ready-now or ready-soon candidate | Track; aim to increase | Succession review |
| Internal fill rate for supervisory positions | Track trend | Workday job change report |
| Vacancy rate in critical job families | Track trend | Position report |
| Retirement-eligible share by job family (aggregate) | Monitor annually | Workday + retirement plan data |
| Workforce plan reviewed with budget | Annually | HR Director log |
