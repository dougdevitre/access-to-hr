# Learning, Development & Certifications — Workday County HR Reference

## Overview

County learning has three layers: required training (safety, harassment prevention,
licensing and certification renewals), job skills, and leadership development. Required
training is a compliance item — it must be assigned, completed and recorded. The other two
are how a county keeps good people and grows its next supervisors.

> ⚠️ LEGAL NOTE: Educational information only, not legal advice. Whether a specific training
  is legally required depends on the job, funding source and license. Check with your state
  labor agency or an employment attorney — for a county, usually the county counselor — and
  the licensing body for each certification.

## Required Training Inventory

Build one list of every required training, with its authority and frequency.

| Training | Typical audience | Authority / owner |
|---|---|---|
| Harassment and discrimination prevention | All employees; supervisors separately | County policy; best practice under EEO law |
| Workplace safety and job-specific safety | Field, maintenance, public works | County safety program, WC carrier or pool requirements |
| Peace officer continuing education | Licensed peace officers | **Missouri** POST — see references/public-safety-hr.md |
| Firefighter / EMS certifications | Fire, EMS | State and national certifying bodies |
| Commercial driver's license (CDL) and drug/alcohol program | CDL drivers | `[VERIFY: FMCSA drug and alcohol testing training requirements for supervisors, 49 CFR 382.603]` |
| Information security awareness | All system users | County IT policy; CJIS for criminal justice access |
| Sunshine Law / records | Records custodians, supervisors | County policy |
| Grant-specific training | Grant-funded positions | Grant agreement |

## Workday Learning Setup

| Task | Path |
|---|---|
| Create a course | Learning > Create Course (blended or digital) |
| Assign to a group | Learning > Learning Campaigns > Create |
| Track due dates | Campaign due date + reminder notifications |
| Record external training | Learning > Record External Learning |
| Certifications | Worker profile certifications, with expiration dates |

> Navigation based on standard Workday HCM. Tenant customization varies — verify in your environment.

### Campaign Checklist

1. Audience defined by eligibility rule (job profile, location, union unit), not by a hand list
2. Due date and two reminders (14 days and 3 days before)
3. Manager notification for overdue employees
4. Completion report scheduled weekly during the campaign
5. Training time recorded as hours worked for non-exempt employees (see references/time-tracking-payroll.md)

## Certification and License Tracking

- Store each certification with issue date, expiration date and issuing body
- Report: certifications expiring in the next 90 days, by department, monthly
- Set the job profile's required certifications so gaps show at hire and on transfer
- Decide in policy what happens when a required license lapses (reassignment, unpaid leave,
  separation) and apply it consistently; involve labor relations for union positions

## Skills and Career Development

| Program | Description |
|---|---|
| Individual development plans | Manager and employee agree on 1–3 goals each year (Performance > Goals) |
| Tuition assistance | Written policy: eligible courses, caps, repayment if the employee leaves `[VERIFY: tax treatment of educational assistance, IRS Publication 15-B]` |
| Job rotation and stretch assignments | Short assignments in another department to build skills |
| Mentoring | Pair new supervisors with experienced ones for 6–12 months |
| Internal certifications | County-specific skills (permitting system, budgeting) |

## Supervisor and Leadership Development

New supervisors in county government often move up from technical roles. A core program:

| Module | Topics |
|---|---|
| The county employment framework | Civil service, MOUs, position control, Sunshine Law |
| Hiring | Structured interviews, questions to avoid — references/recruiting.md |
| Performance and discipline | Coaching, PIPs, just cause — references/performance.md |
| Leave and accommodation | When to call HR — references/leave-absence.md, references/employee-relations.md |
| Time and pay | FLSA, overtime approval, comp time — references/time-tracking-payroll.md |
| Labor relations | Weingarten, grievances, contract administration — references/labor-relations.md |
| Workday for managers | Approvals, team reports, mobile app |

Target: complete within the supervisor's first 6 months.

## Measuring Learning

| KPI | Target | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Required training completion by due date | >95% | Workday learning campaign report |
| Certifications expired while still assigned to the job | 0 | Certification expiration report |
| New supervisors completing core program within 6 months | >90% | Learning report |
| Employees with a development goal | Track; aim to increase | Goals report |
| Internal promotion rate | Track trend | Workday job change report |
| Training spend per employee | Track trend | Finance |
