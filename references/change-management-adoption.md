# Change Management & Workday Adoption — Workday County HR Reference

## Overview

Most Workday problems in a county are adoption problems: managers who still email HR
instead of approving in Workday, employees who call payroll instead of checking their pay
slip, and paper forms that never went away after go-live. Change management is the planned
work of helping people move from the old way to the new one and making the new way stick.

---

## Why County Change Is Different

| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Elected officials and appointed department heads | Authority is distributed; one department can opt out in practice |
| Unions | Changes to working conditions may need notice or bargaining — see references/labor-relations.md |
| Long-tenure workforce | Deep knowledge of old processes; skepticism of "new systems" |
| Field and shift workers | Limited computer access; need mobile and kiosk options |
| Public scrutiny | Implementation problems become news and board agenda items |

---

## A Simple Change Framework

1. **Why:** a one-paragraph reason for the change that an employee would repeat
2. **Who:** stakeholder map — who is affected, how much, who influences them
3. **What changes for me:** by role (employee, manager, HR, payroll)
4. **When:** timeline with clear cutover dates
5. **How I learn:** training by role, short and just in time
6. **Where I get help:** one help channel, known to everyone
7. **How we know it worked:** adoption metrics (below)
8. **Reinforce:** stop accepting the old way after a set date

---

## Stakeholder Map

| Group | Concern | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Elected officials / board | Cost, risk, public perception | Short briefings; adoption metrics; service outcomes |
| Department heads | Workload, control | Department champions; department-level dashboards |
| Managers / supervisors | "One more thing to do" | Show time saved; mobile approvals; manager training |
| Employees | Pay accuracy, privacy, ease | Pay slip walkthrough; mobile app; help desk |
| Union leadership | Working conditions, notice | Early notice; involve in testing where appropriate |
| HR and payroll staff | Job changes, new skills | Role clarity, training, recognition |

## Training by Role

| Role | Training | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Employees | Pay slip, time off request, personal data, benefits, mobile app | 10–15 minute videos; one-page guides; kiosk sessions for field staff |
| Managers | Approvals, team time, time off, hiring requests, performance reviews | 60-minute live session + job aids |
| HR partners | Business processes, reports, troubleshooting | Hands-on sandbox sessions |
| Payroll | Time to pay, corrections, audits | Vendor + internal |
| Executives | Dashboards, approvals on mobile | 15-minute one-on-one |

Use Workday Learning to assign and track — see references/learning-development.md.

## Adoption Metrics

| Metric | How to measure | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Self-service rate | Business processes initiated by employee or manager ÷ all | Track; aim upward each quarter |
| Mobile app usage | Active mobile users ÷ workers | Track trend |
| Manager approvals on time | Approvals within SLA ÷ all | >90% |
| Paper forms still in use | Inventory count | Down to 0 for in-scope processes |
| HR help desk tickets by topic | Ticket system | Falling for trained topics |
| Pay slip questions to payroll | Call/email log | Falling after go-live + 3 months |

## Retiring Paper and Workarounds

1. Inventory every paper form and email workflow in HR and payroll
2. For each: Workday replacement exists? If yes, set a retirement date; if no, log as an optimization item (references/implementation-optimization.md)
3. Announce the date 30 days ahead; remind at 7 days
4. After the date, return paper forms with a link to the Workday task
5. Report remaining workarounds monthly

---

## Communicating the Change

- Lead with what changes for the reader, not with the system name
- Use the HR communication calendar — see references/hr-communications.md
- Have supervisors deliver key messages to field staff in person
- Share early wins (for example, "time off requests now approved in an average of one day")

---

## Resistance — Practical Responses

| What you hear | Likely cause | Response |
|---|---|---|
| "I don't have time" | Process feels slower | Show the mobile path; remove duplicate steps |
| "I don't trust it" | Past pay errors | Fix errors fast; publish accuracy metrics |
| "My department is different" | Real exception or habit | Review; configure if real, hold the line if not |
| "Nobody told me" | Communication gap | Use supervisors and multiple channels |

| KPI | Target | Source |
